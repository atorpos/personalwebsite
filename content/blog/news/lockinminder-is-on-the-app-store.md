### What LockinMinder does

Combination locks are everywhere: the gym locker, the bike rack, the suitcase, the school locker. LockinMinder keeps
their combinations where you need them, and remembers where you snapped each padlock shut.

- **Combinations on your wrist.** Open a lock on Apple Watch and its combination appears in large numbers, one tile
  per wheel. Locks with 3, 4 or 5 numbers from 0 to 99 fit, wheel locks and dial padlocks alike.
- **A map back to your padlock.** Tap **Mark as Locked** and the app saves the time and where you are. Later, the
  lock's screen shows it on a map, and the iPhone app offers walking directions in Apple Maps.
- **On your Lock Screen.** While a lock is locked, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and in
  the Apple Watch Smart Stack shows how long it has been. It never shows the combination.
- **In sync.** Add or edit a lock on any device; changes sync through your own iCloud account.
- **In four languages.** English, Spanish, French and Traditional Chinese.

![Checking a combination on Apple Watch: the list, the lock's combination, and the digits hidden when the wrist is lowered](/projects/lockinminder/flow-2-check-combination.png)

### Private by design

Your locks are kept in your private iCloud database, with each lock's name, combination and location end-to-end
encrypted. LockinMinder has no server of its own. The app opens with Face ID, Touch ID or an app passcode, combinations
never appear in the list, and they turn into dots as soon as you lower your wrist. A location is saved only when you
tap **Mark as Locked**.

### Free and Pro

Up to three locks are free, with their combinations, the map, walking directions and iCloud sync. **Pro** is a
one-time purchase, not a subscription: it unlocks unlimited locks and adds the live distance and a compass arrow back
to your lock in the Live Activity, on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.
