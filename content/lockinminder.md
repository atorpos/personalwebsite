# LockinMinder

Your lock combinations on your wrist, and a map to every padlock you left behind.

The combination lock app for your gym locker, bike lock and suitcase. Check a combination at the lock, tap
**Mark as Locked**, and find your way back to it later on a map. Now on the App Store.

---features[0]
symbol: watch
title: Combinations on your wrist
---

Open a lock on Apple Watch and its combination appears in large numbers, one tile per wheel. No phone, no notes app.

---features[1]
symbol: lock
title: Every kind of combination lock
---

Each lock has a name and a combination of 3, 4 or 5 numbers from 0 to 99, so wheel locks and dial padlocks both fit.

---features[2]
symbol: map
title: Remembers where you locked it
---

**Mark as Locked** saves the time and where you are. The lock's screen shows a map with a pin, and the iPhone app
offers walking directions in Apple Maps.

---features[3]
symbol: bell
title: On your Lock Screen
---

While a lock is locked, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and in the Apple Watch Smart Stack
shows how long it's been. Never the combination.

---features[4]
symbol: cloud
title: In sync through your iCloud
---

Add, edit or delete a lock on any of your devices. Changes sync through your own iCloud account, and **Sync Now**
checks on demand.

---features[5]
symbol: globe
title: Speaks your language
---

LockinMinder is in English, Spanish, French and Traditional Chinese.

---steps[0]
title: Add a lock
image:
  src: /projects/lockinminder/flow-1-add-lock.png
  alt: 'Adding a lock on Apple Watch: a name and how many numbers, then one wheel per number'
---

On Apple Watch, adding a lock takes two steps so the wheels get a whole screen: a name and how many numbers, then one
0–99 wheel per number, like the dials on the real lock. Tap a wheel and turn the Digital Crown to set it. Leave the
name blank and it becomes "Lock 1", "Lock 2" and so on. On iPhone and iPad, it's a single form.

---steps[1]
title: Check a combination at the lock
image:
  src: /projects/lockinminder/flow-2-check-combination.png
  alt: The list of locks, a lock's combination, and the digits hidden when the wrist is lowered
---

The list shows only names and whether each lock is locked, so nobody nearby can read a combination from it. The digits
appear when you open a lock, and turn into dots as soon as you lower your wrist.

---steps[2]
title: Find your padlock again
image:
  src: /projects/lockinminder/flow-3-save-location.png
  alt: Mark as Locked, the location permission asked once, the map on the lock's screen, and the full map
---

After snapping the padlock shut, tap **Mark as Locked**. Your device takes one location fix and saves it with the time.
Later, the lock's screen shows where it is and how accurate the spot is ("Within 40 ft"), and the map shows the lock
and you together. If location is off, the lock is still marked as locked, with the time.

---privacy
title: Private by design
image:
  src: /projects/lockinminder/icloud-sync.png
  alt: Apple Watch and iPhone both sync with your private iCloud database
---

- **The app locks itself.** iPhone and iPad open with Face ID, Touch ID or a 6-digit app passcode. Apple Watch opens
  only while it's unlocked on your wrist.
- **Combinations stay out of sight.** A combination is shown only on its lock's own screen, never in the list, and it
  is hidden on the dimmed Always On display and in the iPhone app switcher.
- **Your data is yours.** Locks are kept in your private iCloud database, with the name, combination and location
  end-to-end encrypted. LockinMinder has no server of its own.
- **No tracking.** A location is saved only when you tap **Mark as Locked**. With Pro, the iPhone follows your location
  only while a lock's Live Activity is showing, to update the distance back to it.

---pricing
title: Free and Pro
---

Up to three locks are free. **Pro** is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it unlocks on every device signed
in to the same Apple Account.

|                                                  | Free                          | Pro                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Saved locks                                      | Up to 3                       | Unlimited                        |
| Combinations, map and walking directions         | Yes                           | Yes                              |
| iCloud sync between Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad | Yes                           | Yes                              |
| Live Activity                                    | Name and how long it's locked | Adds live distance and direction |

With Pro, the Live Activity shows the distance and a compass arrow back to the lock, updated while you walk. iCloud
sync stays free, and locks you've already saved are never hidden or deleted.
